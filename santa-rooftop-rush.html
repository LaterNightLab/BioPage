The Longest Night

It's 23:00 on Christmas Eve and the whole city is asleep. Santa Rooftop Rush is a third-person 3D lane runner across snow-covered rooftops: Santa sprints forward on his own, and it's up to you to dodge, jump, slide and fling presents down every chimney before the sun comes up.

The clock is the real enemy: dawn breaks at 06:00. Fall off the roofs or let the sun rise before the finish line, and the night's mission is lost.

Right Gift, Right Chimney

Chimneys alternate along the edges of the route. When one comes close, the delivery window opens: throw the ◆ red gift to the left, the ▲ green gift to the right. Every correct delivery is worth +200 points.

🎁 Delivery Window

The HUD tells you which gift each chimney is waiting for. A wrong color costs you the points, never the time.

❄️ Rooftop Hazards

Change lane around air conditioners, jump low fences, slide under cables strung with festive lights.

⏰ Night Clock

Only crashing into obstacles costs time: −3.5 seconds of night per hit. Keep it clean to beat the sunrise.

📈 Live Forecast

The festive cable bar projects your final score in real time: deliveries plus the dawn speed bonus.

Chase the S Rank

Reach the finish line and your night is graded from D to S, plus a dawn speed bonus for beating the sunrise. Your best grade and high score are saved locally on your PC, so the only rival is last night's Santa.

Controls

Keyboard and mouse, made for quick reflexes. Restart instantly at any time.

A / D · ← → Change lane across the three rooftop lanes
W · Space · ↑ Jump over low obstacles and fences
S · Shift · ↓ Slide under cables and festive light strings
Q · Left Click Red gift ◆ to the left-side chimney
E · Enter · Right Click Green gift ▲ to the right-side chimney
Esc · P Pause and audio settings
R Restart the run at any moment
